Title: Professor Bubble

Main Photo: professor_bubble_cover.jpg (also for on the ProjectPage in Main.vue)

Hook:

Professor Bubble's laboratory has been thrown into chaos! Navigate
various platforming challenges in the lab and use bubbles to your
advantage by freezing them into solid platforms for a short while.

Description:

This is a game created in 48 hours  for the "Global Game Jam" 2025 at
HKU Netherlands. The theme was "Bubbles". The game got first place in
the jury vote and second place in the public vote.
